HP Tracker for Windows
======================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop, Documents...). Keep "SDL2.dll" next
   to "HP Tracker.exe"; the app needs it.
2. Double-click "HP Tracker.exe".

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app
isn't signed by a paid developer account. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Your encounter saves automatically to:
    %APPDATA%\HotelWorkshop\HPTracker\

Credits, the SRD 5.1 attribution and the licenses of the libraries
the app uses are in CREDITS.txt.

Shortcuts use Ctrl: Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+A select all,
Ctrl+D duplicate, Delete removes the selected cards.
